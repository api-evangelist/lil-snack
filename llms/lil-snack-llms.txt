# Lil Snack

> Lil Snack is a Manhattan Beach, California games company (founded 2024) that builds "snackable" daily games and party games — short pop-culture puzzle, word, and trivia titles that refresh every day. It operates its own consumer destination at lilsnack.com and also powers games embedded inside partner consumer platforms. Lil Snack publishes no public developer API, SDKs, or documentation portal; partner integrations are arranged through business development.

## Games
- [Clue Daily](https://www.lilsnack.com/game/clue): Daily Clue-branded deduction puzzle.
- [Scattergories Daily](https://www.lilsnack.com/game/scattergories): Daily Scattergories, built with Hasbro.
- [Swap](https://www.lilsnack.com/game/swap): Daily tile-swapping word puzzle.
- [Pickle](https://www.lilsnack.com/game/pickle): Daily pop-culture puzzle.
- [All Games](https://www.lilsnack.com/): Full daily lineup, including DownWords, Lite Brite Daily, Wheel of Fortune TODAY, Quiz Climb, Venn, Chain, Stacked, WHAT THE?, Putt Putt Problems, and Quoted.
